# Token Rebinder — Launch Assets

Distribution + recognition kit. The wedge is one claim, provable on any real design system:
**native "Check designs" matches a value to *any* variable that shares it; Token Rebinder
learns how your design actually uses it and binds correctly — and shows its work.**

## 1. Figma Community listing

**Name:** Token Rebinder

**Tagline (≤ 60 chars):**
> The correct binder for AI-pushed designs. Free.

**Short description:**
> Pushed a screen from Claude, Codex, or Figma Make and lost every variable binding?
> Token Rebinder restores them — correctly. It learns how your design *actually uses*

**Full description:**
> **AI code-to-canvas strips your design system.** Frames arrive with hardcoded hex,
> raw spacing, detached text — every variable binding gone.
>
> Figma's native "Check designs" tries to help, but it matches a hardcoded value to
> *any* variable that happens to share it. On a real system that means your brand color
> gets bound to a random component token.
>
> **Token Rebinder is the correct binder.** It scans how your design *actually uses*
> each value and rebinds to the token your team uses most — and it proves it:
>
> - **Watch it learn.** As it scans, each color resolves live onto its real token, the
>   match count climbing (`#0F3549 → Text/Primary ×3,381`).
> - **Review before you apply.** Every proposed binding streams into tabs with a
>   provenance badge — `Learned ×N`, `Exact`, `Fuzzy ±2`, `Semantic`, `Fallback` — so
>   you see *why* each token was chosen. Nothing on your canvas changes until you click Apply.
> - **50 of 51 binding types.** Colors, spacing, radius, typography, effects, layout
>   grids, component props, fonts — not just colors.
> - **Honest.** A Token Health Score receipt and an exact "N applied, M skipped" count.
>
> Free for everyone. Runs on any Figma plan.

## 2. Launch post (X / LinkedIn thread)

> 1/ Figma shipped "Check designs" to auto-bind variables. I ran it on our real design
> system. It mapped our primary brand color → a *map-marker component token*. 🧵
>
> 2/ The problem: it matches a hardcoded value to ANY variable that shares that value.
> `#0F3549` lives in 12 tokens. It picked one at random. On a 600-node screen that's
> dozens of silently-wrong bindings.
>
> 3/ So we built Token Rebinder. Same one-click flow — but it *learns how your design
> actually uses each value* first. `#0F3549` is your text color on 3,381 layers, so it
> binds → `Text/Primary`. Majority rules. [GIF: rows resolving live, ×count climbing]
>
> 4/ And it shows its work. Every proposed binding gets a provenance badge before you
> apply: `Learned ×3381`, `Exact`, `Fuzzy ±2`, `Semantic`, `Fallback`. You review a
> complete plan against an untouched frame. Nothing mutates until you click Apply.
>
> 5/ 50 of 51 binding types — colors, spacing, radius, type, effects, grids, component
> props. Native covers a handful. Free for everyone, any plan.
>
> 6/ If you push screens from Claude / Codex / Figma Make and watch your tokens vanish —
> this is for you. [Community link]
